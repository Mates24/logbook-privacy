DELETE YOUR LOGBOOK ACCOUNT

LogBook – Digital Yacht Log, developed by Mathias Matejčík

Delete it in the app (immediate)

  1. Open LogBook and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all of its data are deleted immediately.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email matejcikmathias@gmail.com with the subject "LogBook account deletion", sent from the email address you registered with. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, country and password
  • All voyages and log entries, including positions, weather, notes and distances
  • Crew members you added, including passport numbers, dates of birth, emergency contacts and medical notes
  • Your saved vessels

Data is removed from our active database at once. Server backups containing it are overwritten within 30 days.

Delete some data but keep your account

You can delete individual records at any time in the app — they are removed from our servers immediately:

  • A voyage (with all its log entries and crew): open the voyage → scroll down → Delete voyage
  • A crew member (including passport and medical details): open the voyage → Crew → remove the crew member
  • A saved vessel: Settings → My vessels → open the vessel → Delete (once no voyage uses it)

To delete a single log entry, or any other specific data, email matejcikmathias@gmail.com from your registered address saying which data to delete; we do it within 30 days.

What is not deleted

  • A GPS track recorded on your phone stays on that device until you uninstall the app.
  • Records of purchases are kept by Apple, Google and our payment provider RevenueCat as required for billing and tax. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Questions? Contact matejcikmathias@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
